FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Ulysses?

For most people, Obsidian is the strongest free alternative to Ulysses for distraction-free Markdown writing. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Ulysses?

Yes. 4 of the 6 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Ulysses?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Typora, iA Writer and Bear Pro cost less than Ulysses. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
