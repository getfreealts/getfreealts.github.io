FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Canva Pro?

For most people, Affinity (by Canva) is the strongest free alternative to Canva Pro for graphic design and social media visuals. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Canva Pro?

Yes. 3 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Canva Pro?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Adobe Express and VistaCreate cost less than Canva Pro. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
