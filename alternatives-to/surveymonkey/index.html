FAQ

What is the best free alternative to SurveyMonkey?

For most people, Google Forms is the strongest free alternative to SurveyMonkey for online surveys. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to SurveyMonkey?

Yes. 4 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to SurveyMonkey?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Fillout, Zoho Survey and Jotform cost less than SurveyMonkey. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
