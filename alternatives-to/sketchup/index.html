FAQ

What is the best free alternative to SketchUp?

For most people, Blender is the strongest free alternative to SketchUp for 3D modeling for architecture and interiors. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to SketchUp?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to SketchUp?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, SketchUp Go, MoI 3D and Plasticity cost less than SketchUp. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free SketchUp alternative for Mac?
