FAQ

What is the best free alternative to MATLAB?

For most people, GNU Octave is the strongest free alternative to MATLAB for numerical computing and engineering. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to MATLAB?

Yes. 6 of the 6 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to MATLAB?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, MATLAB Home and MATLAB Student cost less than MATLAB. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Spyder is our pick for that: mATLAB users moving to Python.
