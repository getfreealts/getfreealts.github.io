FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Tableau?

For most people, Looker Studio is the strongest free alternative to Tableau for data visualization and dashboards. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Tableau?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Tableau?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Power BI Pro and Zoho Analytics cost less than Tableau. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Is there a free Tableau alternative that works in the browser?

Apache Superset is our pick for that: business intelligence dashboards.
