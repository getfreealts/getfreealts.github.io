FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Audible?

For most people, Libby is the strongest free alternative to Audible for audiobooks. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Audible?

Yes. 1 of the 6 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Audible?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Spotify Premium (with audiobooks), Everand and Chirp cost less than Audible. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Audible alternative for Mac?

Spotify (free audiobooks and podcasts) is our pick for that: podcasts and some free audiobooks.

Is there a free Audible alternative that works in the browser?

hoopla is our pick for that: borrowing TV seasons and albums.

Is there a free Audible alternative for iPhone or Android?

LibriVox is our pick for that: classic books as audio.
