FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Adobe Acrobat?

For most people, Stirling-PDF is the strongest free alternative to Adobe Acrobat for editing, merging and signing PDFs. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Adobe Acrobat?

Yes. 7 of the 8 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Adobe Acrobat?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, PDF-XChange Editor (paid), Foxit PDF Editor and PDF Expert cost less than Adobe Acrobat. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
