FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Adobe Premiere Pro?

For most people, DaVinci Resolve (free version) is the strongest free alternative to Adobe Premiere Pro for video editing. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Adobe Premiere Pro?

Yes. 6 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Adobe Premiere Pro?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, DaVinci Resolve Studio, Final Cut Pro, Wondershare Filmora and CyberLink PowerDirector cost less than Adobe Premiere Pro. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Adobe Premiere Pro alternative for Mac?

Shotcut is our pick for that: editing many file formats quickly.
