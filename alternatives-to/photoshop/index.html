FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Adobe Photoshop?

For most people, GIMP is the strongest free alternative to Adobe Photoshop for photo editing and raster graphics. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Adobe Photoshop?

Yes. 3 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Adobe Photoshop?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Adobe Photoshop Elements, Pixelmator Pro and Corel PaintShop Pro cost less than Adobe Photoshop. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Adobe Photoshop alternative for Mac?

Affinity (by Canva) is our pick for that: professional design work without a subscription.

Is there a free Adobe Photoshop alternative that works in the browser?

Photopea is our pick for that: opening and editing PSD files in a browser.

Is there a free Adobe Photoshop alternative for iPhone or Android?

Krita is our pick for that: digital painting and illustration.
