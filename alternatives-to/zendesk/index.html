FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Zendesk?

For most people, Freshdesk (free plan) is the strongest free alternative to Zendesk for customer support and help desk. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Zendesk?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Zendesk?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, FreeScout paid modules, Zoho Desk Standard and Freshdesk Growth cost less than Zendesk. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Is there a free Zendesk alternative that works in the browser?

Zoho Desk (free plan) is our pick for that: tiny support teams.

Is there a free Zendesk alternative for iPhone or Android?

Freshdesk (free plan) is our pick for that: small support teams.
