FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Jira?

For most people, Plane is the strongest free alternative to Jira for issue tracking and project management. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Jira?

Yes. 6 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Jira?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, YouTrack and ClickUp cost less than Jira. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Jira alternative for Mac?

Huly is our pick for that: teams replacing Jira, Slack and Notion together.

Is there a free Jira alternative that works in the browser?

Taiga is our pick for that: agile teams using kanban or scrum.
