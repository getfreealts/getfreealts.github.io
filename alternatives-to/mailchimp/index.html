FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Mailchimp?

For most people, MailerLite (free plan) is the strongest free alternative to Mailchimp for email newsletters and marketing. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Mailchimp?

Yes. 4 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Mailchimp?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, MailerLite Growing Business, Brevo Starter and Sendy cost less than Mailchimp. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Is there a free Mailchimp alternative that works in the browser?

Brevo (free plan) is our pick for that: large contact lists with low sending volume.
