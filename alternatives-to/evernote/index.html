FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Evernote?

For most people, Joplin is the strongest free alternative to Evernote for note-taking and web clipping. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Evernote?

Yes. 6 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Evernote?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Obsidian Sync, UpNote and Bear Pro cost less than Evernote. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Evernote alternative for Mac?

Obsidian is our pick for that: linked notes and personal knowledge management.

Is there a free Evernote alternative that works in the browser?

Standard Notes is our pick for that: secure, simple notes.

Is there a free Evernote alternative for iPhone or Android?

Logseq is our pick for that: outlining and daily journaling.
