FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Adobe Illustrator?

For most people, Inkscape is the strongest free alternative to Adobe Illustrator for vector illustration and logo design. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Adobe Illustrator?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Adobe Illustrator?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Amadine and VectorStyler cost less than Adobe Illustrator. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Adobe Illustrator alternative for Mac?

Affinity (by Canva) is our pick for that: professional design work without a subscription.

Is there a free Adobe Illustrator alternative that works in the browser?
