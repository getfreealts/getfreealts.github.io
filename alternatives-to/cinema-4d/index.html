FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Cinema 4D?

For most people, Blender is the strongest free alternative to Cinema 4D for 3D modeling and animation. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Cinema 4D?

Yes. 3 of the 6 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Cinema 4D?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Houdini Indie and Plasticity cost less than Cinema 4D. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Cinema 4D alternative for Mac?

Houdini Apprentice is our pick for that: learning procedural 3D and effects.
