FAQ

What is the best free alternative to 1Password?

For most people, Bitwarden is the strongest free alternative to 1Password for password management. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to 1Password?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to 1Password?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Bitwarden Premium and Proton Pass Plus cost less than 1Password. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free 1Password alternative for Mac?

KeePassXC is our pick for that: keeping passwords offline.

Is there a free 1Password alternative that works in the browser?

Google Password Manager is our pick for that: chrome and Android users.

Is there a free 1Password alternative for iPhone or Android?

KeePassDX is our pick for that: keePass on Android.
