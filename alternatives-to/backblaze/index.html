FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Backblaze?

For most people, Kopia is the strongest free alternative to Backblaze for computer backup. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Backblaze?

Yes. 6 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Backblaze?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Backblaze B2 or Wasabi with Kopia or restic and IDrive cost less than Backblaze. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
