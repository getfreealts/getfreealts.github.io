FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Salesforce?

For most people, HubSpot CRM (free) is the strongest free alternative to Salesforce for customer relationship management (CRM). The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Salesforce?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Salesforce?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Bigin by Zoho CRM, Zoho CRM (paid plans), Pipedrive and HubSpot Starter CRM cost less than Salesforce. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Is there a free Salesforce alternative that works in the browser?

Zoho CRM (free plan) is our pick for that: very small sales teams.

Is there a free Salesforce alternative for iPhone or Android?

HubSpot CRM (free) is our pick for that: small teams starting with a CRM.
