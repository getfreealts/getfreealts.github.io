FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Squarespace?

For most people, WordPress is the strongest free alternative to Squarespace for building websites. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Squarespace?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Squarespace?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, GitHub Pages with your own domain, Carrd Pro and WordPress.com Personal cost less than Squarespace. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

What is the best free Squarespace alternative for Mac?

Hugo is our pick for that: fast sites and blogs for technical users.

Is there a free Squarespace alternative that works in the browser?

Google Sites is our pick for that: simple free websites.
