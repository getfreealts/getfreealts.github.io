What is the best free alternative to Todoist?

For most people, Microsoft To Do is the strongest free alternative to Todoist for to-do lists and task management. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Yes. 5 of the 6 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, TickTick Premium and Things 3 cost less than Todoist. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
