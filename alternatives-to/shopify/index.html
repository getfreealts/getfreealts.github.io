FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Shopify?

For most people, WooCommerce is the strongest free alternative to Shopify for running an online store. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Shopify?

Yes. 6 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Shopify?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, Shopify Starter, Payhip, Big Cartel and Ecwid cost less than Shopify. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.

Is there a free Shopify alternative that works in the browser?

Square Online (free plan) is our pick for that: small sellers who want hosting handled.

How do you rank these tools?

We weigh features, how actively each project is maintained (pulled from GitHub daily), platform support and ease of use.
