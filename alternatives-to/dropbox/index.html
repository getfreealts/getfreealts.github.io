FAQ

What is the best free alternative to Dropbox?

For most people, Google Drive (15 GB free) is the strongest free alternative to Dropbox for cloud file storage and sync. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to Dropbox?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to Dropbox?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, pCloud Lifetime, Google One 100 GB and Microsoft 365 Basic cost less than Dropbox. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
