FAQ

What is the best free alternative to AutoCAD?

For most people, FreeCAD is the strongest free alternative to AutoCAD for 2D and 3D CAD drafting. The right pick depends on your workflow, so compare the platforms and activity in the table above.

Is there an open-source alternative to AutoCAD?

Yes. 5 of the 7 tools listed here are open source, meaning their code is public and free to use.

Is there a cheaper paid alternative to AutoCAD?

Yes. If you want more than the free tools offer, BricsCAD Lite, progeCAD and ZWCAD cost less than AutoCAD. Prices are approximate, so check each vendor's site.
