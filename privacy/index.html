Privacy policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

QuitPayingFor is a free directory of software alternatives. We don't ask you to sign up, and we don't sell your personal information. This page explains what is collected when you visit.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: pages visited, how long people stay, roughly which country and type of device they use, which site sent them here, and which outbound links they click. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. We see these numbers as totals and trends, not as information about you personally.

If we show ads, they are served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings. Learn how Google uses data from sites like ours at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Some links may be affiliate links. If you buy something through one, the shop may record that the visit came from QuitPayingFor.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works without cookies.

Other websites

We link to many other websites. Their privacy practices are their own; please check their policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email us at quitpayingfor@gmail.com.
