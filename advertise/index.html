How booking works

  1. Check the spot is Available in the table below.
  2. Click the Pay button for your package, pick your page or category from the list (only free spots are shown), and enter your app name and website.
  3. That's it: your spot goes live automatically, usually within 15 minutes, and runs for 30 days. Your card shows your app name and your website's own description. Want a different description, a later start date or an invoice? Email quitpayingfor@gmail.com.

Your link carries tracking tags (utm_source=quitpayingfor), so you can see QuitPayingFor visits in your own analytics. Ask us for the clicks we recorded at the end of your run.

The rules

  • Sponsored spots are always labelled Sponsored and never change our rankings or reviews.
  • Your app must be relevant to the page: a real alternative or tool for the same job.
  • No misleading claims, adult content, gambling, crypto schemes or malware. We may remove or decline any listing; you're refunded in full if we can't run it.
  • Sponsored links use rel="sponsored", as search engines require.
